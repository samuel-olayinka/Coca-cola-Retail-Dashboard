# Coca-cola-Retail-Dashboard
Excel Dashboard analyzing Coca-cola USA Retail Sales From 2022-2023
---

## Project Overview

The dashboard aims to provide business stakeholders with insights into Coca-Cola’s sales trends by brand, retailer, and region. It helps track performance over time and identify high-growth areas for strategic planning.

---

## Features
 Total Sales, Units Sold, Average Price, and Profit  
- Breakdown by Beverage Brand and Retailer  
- Sales and Operating Profit Margin by Quarter  
- Interactive Region and Year slicers  
- Dynamic visualizations created with Excel charts & formulas

---

## Tools Used

- *Microsoft Excel* (Dashboard design and visualization)  
- *Pivot Tables and Charts*  
- *Slicers for filtering by Region and Year*
  
---
## Aims & Objectives

- Visualize Coca-Cola’s retail sales growth from 2022 to 2023  
- Identify top-selling brands and high-performing retailers  
- Track quarterly profit margins and sales fluctuations  
- Make the data accessible and interactive using Excel  

---

## Dataset

Included file: Coca Cola Dataset 2.xlsx  
Contains:
- Sales data (2022 & 2023)  
- Retailer names  
- Beverage brand categories  
- Regional sales info  
- Unit price, quantity sold, profit  

---

## Limitations

- Dataset limited to only two years  
- Does not include promotional, customer, or inventory data

---

## Problems Faced

- Creating dynamic and clean visual layouts in Excel  
- Managing large data sets with slicers and pivot tables  
- Ensuring slicers and charts update correctly across sheets  
- Designing readable visualizations within Excel limitations  

---

## How to Use

1. Open the Excel file Coca Cola Dataset 2.xlsx  
2. Navigate to the Dashboard sheet  
3. Use the slicers on the left to filter by Region or Year  
4. Explore the charts to view brand and retailer performance
